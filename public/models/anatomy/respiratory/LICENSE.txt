MED3D modifications: curated selection, OBJ-to-GLB conversion, common mapping
(x,y,z) -> (x,z,-y)/1000, Float32 positions and Meshopt compression. No extra
decimation, deformation, per-piece placement, generated anatomy or mirroring.
Pulmonary segments are grouped into lungs/lobes without cutting or merging.
Original bytes, individual hashes, identities and transformations are preserved
in research/anatomy/respiratory-originals.zip and respiratory-source-manifest.json.

Educational viewer only. No clinical validation or physiological simulation.
