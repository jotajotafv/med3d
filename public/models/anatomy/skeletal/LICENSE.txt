MED3D modifications:
- Selected 206 original source elements covering 199 standard adult bones and 4 accessory foot sesamoids.
- Removed FJ2772 only after verifying that all ordered vertex, normal and triangle lines exactly duplicate the retained hyoid FJ3201.
- Kept 205 separate identified meshes; the sternum retains 3 named components.
- Converted common coordinates from source millimetres (Z superior, X anatomical left, Y posterior) to metres/Y-up: (x,y,z) -> (x,z,-y)/1000. No bone or region was independently centered, mirrored, repositioned or generated.
- Converted to GLB, added a neutral visualization material, and separated seven regional modules without altering source triangles.
- Used Meshopt compression with 16-bit position quantization and 12-bit normals; no additional decimation.
- Added Spanish editorial display names and a navigational hierarchy; retained original English labels, FMA concepts and source element IDs.

Coverage remains incomplete: a separately identifiable coccyx and six auditory ossicles are absent. No absent structure was synthesized or represented by a primitive.
The source is a reference adult male and does not depict individual variability or clinical-quality microanatomy.

Per-file source/output checksums, counts and modifications: source-manifest.json, catalog.json and validation.json.
